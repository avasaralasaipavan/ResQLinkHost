Privacy & security

ResQLink is built around one principle: your emergency contacts are private by design.

QR codes expose nothing sensitive

The QR code contains only a random, non-guessable link. No name, email or phone number is ever encoded in it.

Helpers must verify themselves

Before a helper can view your contacts they must prove they control an email address or mobile number via a one-time OTP code.

Full transparency

Every verified access is logged with the helper’s identity, time and outcome. You can review the log from your dashboard at any time.

You stay in control

You choose the maximum number of accesses allowed in a time window, and you can deactivate or regenerate your QR whenever you like.

Signals & storage

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. OTP codes are stored hashed, expire quickly, limit failed attempts and are subject to rate limiting.

This app is a student engineering project; treat it as a demonstration of the concept.
